Who We Are

ENGINEERING STRENGTH.
Crafted with precision.

MNS Steel Fabrication is a steel and metal fabrication workshop based at C-257, Kalindi Kunj, New Delhi, serving residential, commercial and industrial requirements across Delhi NCR. Our work spans steel structures, car parking sheds, industrial and warehouse sheds, factory roofing, gates, grills, staircases, balcony railings and custom metalwork — each built around the project at hand.

We take a practical approach to fabrication: understand the site, discuss design and specifications, and deliver metalwork that fits the requirement — with attention to finishing, fit and durability.

  • Steel fabrication for residential, commercial and industrial projects
  • Car parking sheds, industrial sheds and warehouse steel structures
  • Custom gates, staircases, railings and architectural metalwork

Common Questions

FREQUENTLY asked.

What fabrication services does MNS offer?
Steel structures, gates and grills, sheds and canopies, staircases, balcony railings, custom fabrication and elevator & lift fabrication — for residential, commercial and industrial requirements across Delhi NCR.
Can I enquire about customised steel structures?
Yes. Steel structures are built around your site measurements and specifications. Share your location, approximate size and purpose on WhatsApp to discuss a suitable structure.
Do you provide car parking sheds?
Yes. Car parking sheds are part of our sheds and canopies work, for homes, offices and commercial premises. Shed sizes and designs are customised to your requirement.
Can shed sizes and designs be customised?
Yes. Industrial sheds, warehouse sheds and canopies are sized to your plot and use. Roofing choices, spans and heights are discussed per project.
Do you offer polycarbonate roofing solutions?
Yes. Polycarbonate sheds and roofing solutions are available where daylight and weather protection are needed. Share your area and design to discuss options.
How can I request a project quotation?
Use the consultation form or message us on WhatsApp with your requirement, location and measurements or drawings. Pricing is discussed per project based on design, materials and scope.
