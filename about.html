Company Introduction

FABRICATION THAT STARTS from your requirement.

MNS Steel Fabrication is based at C-257, Kalindi Kunj, New Delhi, and serves customers across Delhi NCR. We work in steel and metal — from structural frameworks and industrial sheds to the gates, staircases and railings that complete a building.

Our listings cover steel fabrication, metal fabrication, car parking sheds, industrial shed fabrication, polycarbonate sheds, steel warehouse sheds, factory roofing, prefabricated structures and custom fabrication. In practice, that means one workshop able to take a project from a conversation about requirements to fabricated, finished metalwork installed at your site.

  • Steel & metal fabrication for homes, commercial spaces and industrial sites
  • Sheds, canopies and prefabricated structures for every type of plot
  • Architectural metalwork — gates, grills, staircases and railings

Workmanship Approach

HOW WE work.

A steel structure is only as good as its fabrication. Every project at MNS moves through the same practical sequence:

  • Understand the requirement — site, size, use and design are discussed before any steel is cut.
  • Discuss design & specifications — measurements, materials and finishes are agreed per project.
  • Fabricate & prepare — cutting, welding and preparation of components to the agreed design.
  • Coordinate & deliver — installation and site coordination; scope and timelines follow project specification and agreement.
