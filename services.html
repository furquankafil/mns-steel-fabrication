01 · Steel Structures
Service 01

Steel Structures

Structural steelwork planned around your site — framework, prefabricated structures and warehouse steelwork.

From steel framework and prefabricated steel structures to warehouse structures, our structural fabrication covers commercial and industrial requirements. A steel structure's quality is decided before fabrication starts: site measurements, spans, heights, loads and the way the structure will be used determine the design. We discuss these project-specific requirements with you — dimensions, structure type, roofing and cladding needs, and site conditions — so the fabrication matches the structure you actually need.

Typical Applications

  • Steel framework for buildings and industrial spaces
  • Prefabricated steel structures for warehouses and sheds
  • Warehouse steel structures and structural fabrication
  • Commercial and industrial structural requirements

Project Considerations

  • Measurements and site conditions drive design and specifications
  • Structural planning is agreed per project before fabrication

Gates & Grills

Custom steel gates and grills — where design, security and finishing meet.

A gate is the first thing a visitor sees and the everyday point of entry for the people who live or work there. We fabricate custom steel gates, main entrance gates, security grills and decorative metal grills for residential and commercial requirements. Design, appearance, utility and finish are discussed together: opening type, panel pattern, height, and the finish that suits your space and neighbourhood.

Typical Applications

  • Custom steel gates and main entrance gates
  • Security grills and decorative metal grills
  • Residential metalwork and commercial entry solutions

Project Considerations

  • Opening size, swing or sliding requirements and site access
  • Finish and detailing matched to the building's character

03 · Sheds & Canopies
Service 03

Sheds & Canopies

Covered steel structures for parking, storage, production and outdoor space — sized to your plot.

Our sheds and canopies work includes car parking sheds, industrial sheds, factory roofing, warehouse sheds, polycarbonate sheds, outdoor canopies and wall-mounted covers, along with customised shed structures. Roofing choices — metal sheeting, polycarbonate for daylight, or a combination — and structural requirements such as span, height and fixing are discussed per project, so the shed fits the site, the use and the budget.

Typical Applications

  • Car parking sheds for homes, offices and institutions
  • Industrial sheds, factory roofing and warehouse sheds
  • Polycarbonate sheds, outdoor canopies and wall-mounted covers

Project Considerations

  • Plot size, span and height define the structure
  • Roofing material chosen for light, weather and use

07 · Elevator & Lift Fabrication
Service 07

ELEVATOR & LIFT METAL FABRICATION

Custom metal fabrication solutions for elevator and lift-related architectural requirements, with attention to appearance, dimensions, material selection, and project specifications.

We fabricate the metal framework and architectural metal elements around lift installations — designed around your shaft opening, floor levels, and the finish you want the lift area to carry. Share your dimensions, reference photos, or drawings and we will discuss a structure that fits your building.

Typical Applications

  • Elevator metal frames.
  • Lift-related metal framework.
  • Glass and metal architectural elements.
  • Custom metal panels.
  • Elevator-related fabrication components.
  • Project-specific metalwork.

Project Considerations

  • Shaft dimensions, floor heights and structural fixing points
  • Material selection, glass/metal combination and surface finish
  • Please note: MNS provides metal fabrication only — lift machinery, elevator installation, maintenance, and certified elevator safety engineering are outside our scope and must be handled by a licensed lift provider.
